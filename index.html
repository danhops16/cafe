About us

Allstar Eateries is your neighborhood spot in Windsor for great coffee, loaded donuts, and savory comfort food made to satisfy any craving.

Whether you are grabbing a quick drink, meeting friends, or stopping in for a late-night bite, we focus on friendly service, fresh flavors, and a welcoming space every day from 6 AM to midnight.
