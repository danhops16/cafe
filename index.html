About Allstar

Your neighbourhood café & kitchen

Allstar Eateries is where Windsor comes for great coffee in the morning, loaded donuts by afternoon, and hearty African comfort food late into the night.

We blend café culture with bold kitchen flavours — pepper soups, jollof plates, nachos, wings, and fresh-baked treats — all served with friendly, unhurried hospitality in a space that feels like home.

Open Monday–Saturday from 9:30 AM to 10:00 PM, and Sunday from 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
